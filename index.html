How MyNegosyo works

Everything your negosyo needs, in one place.

MyNegosyo helps you manage your products, sales, services, utang, GCash, e-load, bills, payroll, and cash from one innovative platform.

01

Manage your inventory

Add your products with prices, costs, stock quantities, reorder levels, barcodes, and expiry dates. Keep track of what is available and what needs to be restocked.

02

Record every transaction

Record your daily sales, services, cash payments, GCash transactions, e-load, and utang. Your records stay organized so you can easily track your business activity.

03

Monitor your negosyo

Use your MyNegosyo dashboard to see sales, inventory, outstanding utang, GCash, bills, payroll, cash vault, and other important business information at a glance.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is MyNegosyo a BIR-accredited POS?

No. This website presents MyNegosyo as a back-office management and digital ledger tool. Businesses remain responsible for applicable registration, invoicing, receipts, and tax compliance.

Can I use it on my phone?

Yes. The UI is designed to be responsive for phones, tablets, and desktop browsers.

Can I track both products and services?

Yes. MyNegosyo can organize product sales and service entries in the same operational workflow.

Can I customize the app for my business?

Yes. The codebase is designed as a web app foundation that can be extended with database, authentication, cloud sync, and custom modules.
